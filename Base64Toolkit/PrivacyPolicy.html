Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-11

This privacy policy applies to the Android app JSON Viewer & JWT Decoder (formerly Base64 Toolkit; earlier Play title "Offline JSON Viewer, Dev Tools"), package com.mobileappzgroup.base64toolkit (the "App"). The App is published by Nishant Chauhan under the name MobileAppzGroup (the "Service Provider") as a freemium service.

Summary

  • Your text, JSON, tokens, files, images and history are processed on your device. We do not receive them and have no servers that store them.
  • The App uses Google services for analytics, crash reports, remote configuration, ads and purchases. These collect limited technical data, described below.
  • Network tools send requests only to hosts you enter yourself.
  • There are no accounts and no sign-in.

Content you process (stays on your device)

The App provides about 130 developer tools (JSON viewer and formatter, JWT decoder, Base64 and other encoders, hashing, regex, time, text, color and QR tools, and more). Anything you type, paste, open or scan is processed locally. Tool history, saved items and settings are stored only on your device (local database and preferences) and are excluded from Android backup. The Service Provider cannot see them. Uninstalling the App deletes them.

Some tools let you paste from or copy to the clipboard. The App reads the clipboard only when you use such an action, and clipboard content is never sent to us or to analytics.

Files and images are opened only through the Android file or photo picker that you operate. The App does not request storage permissions.

Camera

The camera permission is used only by the QR scanner, and only after you choose to scan with the camera. Camera frames are decoded on the device. They are not stored or uploaded. The App works without the camera: you can scan a QR image from the gallery instead.

Information collected automatically by third-party services

The App includes the following Google services. They may collect the data listed, which is processed by Google under its own policies:

  • Firebase Analytics: app interactions such as which tool was opened, purchase and ad events, app version, device model, operating system, approximate region, and a Firebase installation ID. We do not put your input, files, search text or history into analytics events.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash and diagnostic reports (stack traces, device state, app version, installation ID) to fix stability problems.
  • Firebase Remote Config: downloads app settings (for example feature limits and links) and uses the Firebase installation ID.
  • Google AdMob: shows banner ads and optional rewarded ads to users who have not purchased Pro. AdMob may use the Android advertising ID, IP address and approximate location derived from it, and device information to serve and measure ads, including personalised ads where you consent. The App declares the AD_ID permission for this.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): shows a consent form where required (for example in the EEA and UK) and records your consent choice. You can change your choice later in the App's settings where offered.
  • Google Play Billing: processes the one-time "Pro" purchase. Payment details are handled by Google Play; the App never sees your card or account details. The App stores locally whether Pro is unlocked.

Where consent is required, analytics and ads are started only after you have made your choice. You can limit ad personalisation or reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy / Google → Ads.

Network connections made by the App

  • Services above: Firebase, AdMob, UMP and Google Play, as described.
  • Other apps list: the App fetches one public JSON file from mobileappzgroup.github.io to show our other apps. No personal data is sent beyond what any web request contains (such as your IP address, which the host sees).
  • Network tools (for example API client, WebSocket tester, DNS lookup, WHOIS lookup, ping, port check, Gist import): these run only when you start them. The request goes directly from your device to the host, URL or address you entered, and is not routed through or logged by us. The destination you choose will see your IP address and whatever you send, and handles it under its own policy. Network tool content is never sent to analytics. Use these tools only on hosts you own or have permission to test.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data is shared only with the Google services above, with service providers acting for us, or where required by law or to protect rights, safety or against fraud.

Retention and deletion

Content you process is kept only on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Data held by Google services is retained under Google's policies. There are no user accounts. To request deletion of analytics or diagnostic data linked to your installation, email mobileappzgroup+base64toolkit@gmail.com and include the date you installed and your device model if possible. You can also uninstall the App and reset your advertising ID. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will act on it.

Security

Content stays on your device, which reduces exposure. Traffic to Google services uses encryption. Network tools may let you send requests over unencrypted http if you enter such an address; the App warns about this. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes
